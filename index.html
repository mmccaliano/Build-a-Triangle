Keyboard controls

Focus the building space. Press 1, 2, or 3 to choose a side. Use the arrow keys to move its connected sides. Press A / D to turn around its first end; hold Shift to turn around its other end. Press Enter to join nearby open ends. Press U to unjoin its first end; hold Shift to unjoin its other end.
